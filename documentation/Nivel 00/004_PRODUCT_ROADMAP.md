# BIOCORE AI

# PRODUCT ROADMAP

Version: 1.0

Status: Active

Classification: Strategic Product Evolution

---

# Introduction

BIOCORE AI is not being developed as a conventional software application.

It is being developed as a Biomedical Operating System capable of integrating biomedical engineering, multimodal physiological intelligence, artificial intelligence, digital simulation and medical education into a single ecosystem.

This roadmap defines the long-term evolution of the platform.

Each phase builds upon the previous one.

No phase should be skipped.

No future feature should violate the architectural principles established in the System Constitution.

---

# Product Evolution Strategy

BIOCORE AI evolves through ten major generations.

Each generation represents a qualitative leap rather than a collection of new features.

The objective is not to increase functionality.

The objective is to increase physiological intelligence.

---

• EEG Laboratory

• EMG Laboratory

• Respiratory Laboratory

• PPG / SpO₂ Laboratory

• Patient Workspace

• Clinical Reports

• Educational Quizzes

↓

Physiological Features

↓

↓

Everything else

This generation fundamentally changes the architecture of BIOCORE AI.

New Capabilities

Milestone

BIOCORE AI stops analyzing signals.

It starts understanding physiology.

# Guiding Principle

BIOCORE AI should never evolve by accumulating isolated modules.

Every new capability must strengthen the Unified Physiological State.

Every innovation must enrich the Digital Human Twin.

Every algorithm must improve physiological understanding.

Every educational experience must improve biomedical literacy.

Every engineering decision must move the platform closer to becoming the world's most advanced Biomedical Operating System.

---

# Success Definition

The roadmap is considered complete when BIOCORE AI functions as a unified physiological intelligence ecosystem capable of integrating biomedical sensing, multimodal artificial intelligence, digital human simulation, clinical education, biomedical research and connected healthcare into a single coherent platform.

Technology changes.

Biomedical science evolves.

Artificial Intelligence advances.

BIOCORE AI must evolve with them while preserving its foundational principles.
