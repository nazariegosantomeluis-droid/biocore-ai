# Purpose

The Physiological World Model (PWM) is the predictive intelligence layer of BIOCORE AI.

It is responsible for constructing an internal computational representation of human physiology capable of simulating biological evolution through time.

Unlike conventional predictive models that estimate isolated variables, the Physiological World Model represents the organism as an interconnected dynamic system.

Its objective is to answer one fundamental question:

"What is likely to happen next?"

---

# Vision

Traditional biomedical software analyzes measurements.

BIOCORE AI understands physiology.

The Physiological World Model transforms the current physiological state into a continuously evolving virtual organism capable of exploring future scenarios before they occur.

It enables clinicians, researchers and students to experiment with physiology safely inside a computational environment.

---

# Core Philosophy

Physiology is dynamic.

Every physiological change produces consequences across multiple biological systems.

The Physiological World Model continuously estimates these interactions, allowing BIOCORE AI to predict not only outcomes but also the mechanisms that produce them.
