# Purpose

The Artificial Cognitive Architecture (ACA) is the distributed intelligence layer of BIOCORE AI.

Unlike traditional software, BIOCORE AI does not rely on a single Artificial Intelligence model.

Instead, it coordinates a federation of specialized cognitive agents that collaborate to understand physiology, assist clinicians, educate students, support researchers and continuously improve the Digital Human Twin.

Artificial Intelligence is therefore treated as a cognitive ecosystem rather than a chatbot.

---

# Vision

Human physiology cannot be understood by one expert.

Hospitals employ cardiologists, neurologists, pulmonologists, intensivists, radiologists, biomedical engineers and researchers because every specialty contributes unique knowledge.

BIOCORE AI follows the same philosophy.

Every AI specializes.

The system reasons collectively.

# Closing Statement

The Artificial Cognitive Architecture is the cognitive nervous system of BIOCORE AI.

Just as the human brain coordinates billions of neurons to understand and respond to the environment, the Artificial Cognitive Architecture coordinates specialized intelligence to interpret physiology, support decision-making and continuously evolve the Digital Human Twin.
