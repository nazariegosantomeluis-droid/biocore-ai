# Purpose

The Biomedical Knowledge Graph (BKG) is the semantic intelligence layer of BIOCORE AI.

Its objective is to organize every biomedical concept, physiological relationship, clinical entity, educational resource and scientific evidence into a unified interconnected knowledge network.

Rather than storing isolated information, the Knowledge Graph models medicine as a living graph of biological relationships.

Every AI agent, Digital Human Twin visualization, educational module and physiological simulation retrieves contextual information from the Biomedical Knowledge Graph.

---

# Vision

Medicine is not a collection of documents.

It is a network of interconnected concepts.

The Knowledge Graph transforms biomedical information into computable knowledge.

Instead of searching for documents, BIOCORE AI navigates relationships.

# Purpose

The Biomedical Knowledge Fabric (BKF) is the distributed semantic infrastructure of BIOCORE AI.

Rather than storing all biomedical knowledge inside a single graph database, the Knowledge Fabric coordinates multiple specialized knowledge graphs into one coherent semantic ecosystem.

Every graph specializes in a biomedical domain while remaining connected through shared ontologies, semantic relationships and physiological concepts.

The Biomedical Knowledge Fabric acts as the universal knowledge substrate for every intelligent component of BIOCORE AI.
